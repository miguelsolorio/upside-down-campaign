Five designs for the Demodog Alpha

The den boss of the Lab, about 32 by 24 px, where a young demodog is 24 by 20. Each card draws one design with the game's own pixel tools in four fill colors and the ink outline, the same count the young demodog uses: the walk frame, the howl pose it takes at 75, 50 and 25 percent health, and the leap before the pounce.

The pale grey demodog on the left of every card is the game's own frame, for scale. The top row is 4x. The strip under it is the real size, 1x, on a tunnel floor. Each design is facing right. The game draws left by flipping.
