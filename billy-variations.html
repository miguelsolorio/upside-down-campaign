Level 9 boss review

Five looks for Flayed Billy

Billy Hargrove in 1985 is the first boss of stage 3.3, in the food court: the mullet he was proud of, the lifeguard's tan gone grey, dark veins over pale skin and dead white eyes. He never speaks while he fights. Eleven reaches him, the veins fade, and he slumps where he knelt. Every card is drawn by the game's own code on a teen body a size up, in a 24 pixel cell like Grigori's, so he looms over the party. Whichever you pick goes into the game as it is.

On each card the top two rows are 4x. The first is the walk, then Steve and Max beside him at the same scale. The second is the charge's wind-up and run, the grab, then the kneel: pinned, the veins fading, and freed and slumped. The third is the portrait: the thing Eleven finds, then the free man in the six expressions the dialogue box has. The two strips under it are the real size, 1x, on a pale floor and on a dark one.
