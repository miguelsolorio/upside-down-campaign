Chapter 5 art, ahead of the build

The Abyss, the heart and the ending

Stage 5.2 floats broken pieces of Hawkins over a red void under a black sun. Stage 5.3 climbs through the flesh into the heart, where Vecna sits in the Mind Flayer's chest, and ends on the basement in 1987 and a die on the table. All of it is drawn by the game's own pixel tools in the game's palette, in src/gfx/ch5/.

The three scenes are real 256 by 224 frames at 3x, drawn as the stage will draw them: backdrop, tiles, props, sprites. Under them is every tile, mark and pose, each in the neighbors that change it. Nothing here is registered with the game yet.

Three mock scenes

The Abyss with islands, a vine bridge over the void and a hole in the lawn. The heart arena with the spider, Vecna in its chest, a hero on each channel mark and the pulsing glow. The ending, the basement with the party round the table, then THE END over it.

5.2, the Abyss

Backdrop behind the tiles, so it shows through every gap. Heroes on a bridge and an island, a Demogorgon on the porch.

5.3, the heart

Eleven's mark is dim, Will's is lit. Hitting a leg opens the chest and Vecna takes the damage.

The ending

The epilogue's last beat is the die. Then the card: the room dimmed, the table still lit.

The Abyss tiles

Five island floors (lawn, road, road with its line, porch, classroom), each with its edges, corners and its rim over a cliff. The cliff is the rock face under an island's south edge, the under tile tapers into the void, a gap is the drop itself, and the vine bridge runs both ways. The tiles that hang over the void are clear where the void shows, here on a dark red ground.

The heart

Red black flesh floor with veins that beat, rib and vein walls, the arena's ribbed edge that rounds off where the floor turns, the two channel marks (Eleven's hot red triangle over three bars, Will's cool blue diamond round an eye) and the four steps of the glow.

The final boss

The Mind Flayer spider with the chest shut and open, Vecna's fast phase (idle, walk, the curse cast, the sink into the floor), the chest close up, and Joyce with her axe in four directions and five poses.

What the build registers

One line each. Every painter is a function of the same shape as the one it was modelled on.
