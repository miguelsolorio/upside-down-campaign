Level 1 night look, the tileset round

Night tileset variations

Five night versions of the Level 1 map, each with its tiles redrawn: a night palette per material, plus night-only details painted in, such as moon rim light on the pines, dew, glowing plants, spores and moon glints on the water. There is no darkness layer. The only lighting is a small painted warm pool around windows, lamps and bulbs.

C, Glow forest, is the one that shipped, and it is what the game draws now. The dashed card is how the game looked before: daytime art under the retired darkness overlay, drawn here with a copy of that old code. Heroes, enemies and pickups keep their normal art in every card.
