Chapter 4 review

Four looks for the demobat

Demobats are the Upside Down's flyers: a leathery body, wide wings, a long whip tail, and a head that opens in petals like the Demogorgon's. They fly in groups and ignore walls. In 4.3 they swoop at Eddie while he plays his solo on the rooftop, and the sound ring stuns them. Chapter 5 brings them back.

Each card is drawn by the game's own pixel tools, in five fill colors and the ink outline, from above. The top row is 4x: four flap frames (wings up, mid, down, mid), glide, dive with the head open, stunned with three stars, and the white hit flash. Under it is the Blue storm street at the size the game shows, with five bats flapping and one diving at Eddie, and the same street at 2x.
