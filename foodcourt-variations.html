Level 9 setting review

Four looks for the food court

Stage 3.3 ends in the food court of Starcourt, at night, under the Confetti mall the owner picked: the arena under the skylight where Billy and the Mind Flayer are fought, the kitchens behind the stalls, and the escalators up from the lower concourse. Each card is the eight new tiles (foodfloor, foodband, skyfloor, stallcap, stall, kitchenfloor, fryer, escalator) plus tables and goo, painted with the game's own pixel tools and palette, one painter per tile name, so whichever you pick goes into the game as it is.

Each look is a corner of the arena (stall fronts with menu boards under their caps, the colored band, the patch of floor under the skylight, tables, goo), a kitchen and a run of escalator, at 2x. Everything goes through the game's night art, and the menu boards, the moon through the skylight, the fryers and the goo get the same dithered pools of light the game paints round a lamp. Under them each tile one by one at 3x, next to the Confetti mall's own, then the three rooms at their real size.
