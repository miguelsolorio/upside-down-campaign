Level 8 boss review

Five looks for Grigori

Grigori is the Soviet enforcer who waits with the Key: a big, cold, blond man, and the boss of stage 3.2. He never speaks, so he has no portrait. Every card is drawn by the game's own code on the big body, Hopper's, in five colors and an ink outline. Whichever you pick goes into the game as it is.

On each card the top row is 4x. The two strips under it are the real size, 1x, on a pale floor and on a dark one. Each design shows him facing down and up, a walk in profile and from the front, the haymaker's wind up and swing, and the grab from the side and the front. Mike, Hopper and Grigori stand side by side at the right, for scale.
