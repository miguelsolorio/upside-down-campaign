HUD review

Header layout variations

Eight ways to lay out what sits beside the active hero: name and level, XP, MP, coins and the Crit meter. Health is not here, since each hero wears an HP bar in the world. All of them use the space the strip has left, which is x 25 to 191, and every one is drawn by the game's own HUD code over the real bench.

Chosen: 8, Captions. It is now in the game. The first card is the header as it was before, redrawn here so you can still compare. What it got wrong, and what the others tried to fix: the labels were 3 by 5 px and dim, the XP notches were 2 px and nearly the color of the strip, the three groups sat at uneven gaps, and the Crit die and its label were the same grey as the empty bars, so it read as switched off.

  • Free: x 25 to 191, the bench starts at 193
