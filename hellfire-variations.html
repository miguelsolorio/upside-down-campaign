The Hellfire Club

Stage 4.2 opens with Eddie's recruit scene: the Hellfire Club meets after hours in a classroom at Hawkins High, spring 1986. Each card is one sample room, 16 by 10 tiles, painted four ways: a chalkboard, windows, a door and the club's banner on the north wall, a linoleum floor, desks in rows on the left, and in the middle the D&D table with the DM screen, a map and minis, Eddie standing at its head and Mike and Dustin in chairs beside him for scale.

The big picture is 3x. The same room at its real size, every tile and the props are on the right of each card. Flames, lamps and lightning flicker.
