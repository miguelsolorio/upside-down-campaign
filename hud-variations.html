HUD review

Level progress, specials and coins

Ten ways to draw the three things that sit around the HP bar: how far you are to the next level, how much you have left for the special, and how many quarters you are carrying. Each card designs all three together, so they share one look.

Chosen: 1, Quiet. It is now in the game. Its card here is a redraw made for this sheet, and the first card is the old HUD, redrawn so you can still compare. The HP bar is the shipped one in every card, pixel for pixel. The portrait, name, bench and d20 meter are drawn by the game's own HUD code. Only the level, MP and quarter pieces change from card to card.

  • Strip: 256 by 20 px, the left panel is x 0 to 158
  • Special cost: 5 to 8 MP, so a bar can count casts
  • Inspiration: the Nat 20 timer lives in the same column as the quarters
