Chapter 3 setting review

Five looks for Starcourt

Each card is one sample mall room for stage 3.1 beside the matching base room for 3.2, 16 by 10 tiles each: floor and band, cap and wall, a lit shop, a shut shop, glass doors, the fountain, a planter, the Scoops Ahoy counter and goo; then grating, ammo crates, a bunk, a cell, the Key's machine, a cipher door with a Cyrillic keypad, a power box plate and a breaker switch. Everything is painted with the game's own pixel tools and palette.

Your pick decides the time of day of both stages. Atrium and Chrome are by day. Neon, Brass and Confetti are after closing, and run through the game's night art, so the neon signs are the only light.

The big pictures are 2x. The tiles and props are under them, then both rooms at their real size. The machine runs. Fire the power box to see it live.
