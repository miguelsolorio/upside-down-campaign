Party picks review

Five layouts for the party picks

From stage 2.2 on, more heroes can play than fit in a party, so the map asks who goes. Each card draws the panel over the real map screen with the game's own renderer, font and palette. The big picture is 3x. The copy beside it is 1x, the size the game draws.

Every card shows the same moment: stage 2.2, six heroes, Mike leading Dustin, Lucas and Max, Max locked in, the cursor on Dustin and START live. The map's own hints are off while a panel is open, because the panel prints its own legend.

The layouts

Pick one by name. The one you choose is what gets built.

The marks are the same in all five

  • An amber frame and LEAD for the lead, the same amber as the active hero in the HUD.
  • A number for the rest of the party, in party order, and a padlock after it for a hero the stage needs.
  • Bone frames for the party, slate for the bench, and dimmer names and levels on the bench.
  • White corners for the cursor, as on the map. The list uses the menus' yellow pointer instead.
  • The gate word is what the hero opens: DASH, SMASH, BURN, SHOOT or SENSE. Mike has none.
  • START is yellow and live with four picked. It would go slate with fewer.
